The opening

The best part of a wedding card was always opening it.

So we kept that moment. Every design starts as a real-looking card on the guest's screen, and their own touch opens it.

Step one

A link arrives

You send one link to the family group. It opens straight in the phone's browser, no app to install.

Step two

They press the seal

A gold ring fills under their thumb while they hold. Then the wax cracks and falls away.

Step three

The card unfolds

The flap lifts and the invitation opens on a golden-hour garden.

Step four

Your names, in full light

Then they scroll down through the date, the events and the map, and tell you they are coming with one tap.
